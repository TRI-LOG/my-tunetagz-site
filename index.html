Got Questions?

FAQ

How do I order a custom TuneTagZ keychain? +

DM us on Instagram @tune.tagz with your Spotify song link and name. We confirm your order and ship within 5–7 working days across India.

How does the Spotify code keychain work? +

Open the Spotify app, tap the camera icon, and scan the engraved code on your keychain. It instantly plays the song it was made with.

How much does a TuneTagZ keychain cost? +

The Spotify Code Tag is ₹699 per piece. The Rocky Keychain is ₹300 per piece. Both ship across India with tracked delivery.

How long does shipping take? +

We offer tracked shipping across India with delivery in 5–7 working days from order confirmation.

Can I customize the name on the keychain? +

Yes — the Spotify Code Tag is fully personalized with your chosen song and your name engraved on the back. Just send us your Spotify link and name when ordering.
